# Civitai SAFE Handoff

SAFE Handoff is an on-site Civitai App Block. Use it to send a workflow, image, supported 3D file, or ZIP to collaborators in their existing browser. Each recipient gets their own offer and verified pickup receipt.

| Start here | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](Getting-Started) | Send, pick up, and verify a file. |
| [Pickup and expiry](Pickup-and-Expiry) | Receipts, deletion timing, and who can use an offer. |
| [Compatibility](Compatibility) | Supported formats, sizes, and direct network requirements. |
| [Review and verification](Review-and-Verification) | Screenshots, exact-build evidence, and the reviewer workflow. |

Sign in on your preferred Civitai domain and open SAFE Handoff if your account has Apps beta access. Keep the sender and recipient tabs open until the pickup and receipt finish. The [review kit](https://chriscollins500.github.io/civitai-safe-handoff/review/#live-deployment) identifies the last verified App Block deployment.
