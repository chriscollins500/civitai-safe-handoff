How it works

One file, separate pickups.

  1. Prepare the file.Choose the files and enter the people who should receive them.
  2. Exchange the offer and reply.Send each offer in private Civitai chat. Connect using that person’s reply.
  3. Verify the saved copy.Each recipient saves the file, then selects it again for the SHA-256 check.

Verified pickup receipts

The last verified save ends the handoff.

Opening an offer, receiving the bytes, or starting a save leaves the pickup open. The receipt arrives after the recipient selects their saved file and its SHA-256 matches.

On expiry, the app closes the pickup and clears its copy from active tabs. A copy already saved to a device stays there.
