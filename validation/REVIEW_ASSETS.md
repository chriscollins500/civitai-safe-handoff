## Cover prompt

> Create a wide 16:9 landscape, premium editorial product photograph for the Civitai SAFE Handoff web project's cover. A visual metaphor of directly sending one file from one creator to several, in a believable physical studio tabletop macro shot. In the right half, a single upright frosted glass archival card with a folded upper corner and no markings, nested into a narrow brushed aluminum base. Three evenly separated smaller glass cards recede toward the background right with subtle mint light catching their edges. One luminous translucent mint reflection on the desk ties the objects together. Sophisticated midnight-blue gradation, slate, deep ink and soft mint #91eac9, occasional titanium highlights, minimal cinematic volumetric contrast, polished but physically realistic optical detail. Wide understated negative space on the left intentionally empty for a title that will be added in HTML. The design should feel like product photography for a creative tool. Leave lower edges clean for responsive crop. No words, letters, numbers, logos, crests, padlocks, people, rendered GUI, fake screens or borders. Center-right objects, full composition readable at small thumbnail scale, precise use of cinematic light.
